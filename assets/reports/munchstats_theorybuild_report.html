⚠️ TGT-01 / GREED: Zap Cannon Variance

While Zap Cannon guarantees an OHKO on Charizard-Mega-Y and 100% paralysis, its 50% unboosted accuracy poses severe variance risks in high-stakes Swiss matches. If Milotic has not established Coil accuracy boosts, consider Thunderbolt or Volt Switch for tournament consistency.

🛑 TGT-02: Normal/Psychic Ghost Immunity

Both Farigiraf (14.72% usage) and Indeedee-F (14.82% usage) are Normal/Psychic dual types. They possess complete immunity to Shadow Ball from Gholdengo and Shadow Sneak from Ceruledge. Always default to Make It Rain or Bitter Blade into these slots.

Open Rillaboom + Ceruledge into Rain/Earthquake cores (instant Grassy Seed + 50% Ground reduction), or Staraptor + Milotic into Incineroar + Sneasler leads (punishing Intimidate with Competitive +2 while intimidating Sneasler).
